Why I Picked CS

As an international student, I don't have much choices when it comes to courses. I hated the thought of handling patients and their bodily fluids, excrements, or shenanigans, so Computer Information Systems seemed like my best bet. Plus, it sounded cool.

Game Dev Would be Sooooo Cool...

Back in 2017, me and my brother pirated "Undertale" because we knew our parents would see it as an unnecessary purchase. The more we played it, the more we were fascinated by how the hell could someone make a game so elaborate. Nine years later, we have plans on making our own spinoff of the game, but we still haven't found the drive to do so. I hope we will, someday.
